<!-- OBJECT -->
<div id="crazy-8s-object">
<div class="rules-box">
<h4><span style="color: #3b82f6;">OBJECT</span></h4>
<div class="rules-box">
<ul>
<li>This game follows the Crazy 8’s style of “Card Shedding” play.</li>
<li>Therefore: Be the first Player to get rid of all your Cards.</li>
</ul>

<!-- SETUP -->
<div id="crazy-8s-setup">
<div class="rules-box">
<h4><span style="color: #3b82f6;">SETUP</span></h4>
<div class="rules-box">
<ol>
<li>As a group choose whether to use the Core Game Extension and any Optional Expansion or Coloured Extension Packs.
<div class="note-box">
<strong>NOTE:</strong>
<ul>
<li>If you have added any optional Expansions, check if they have any Additional Setup Rules.</li>
</ul>
</div>
</li>
<li>Choose who <a href="#glossary:deal">Deals</span></strong></a> first [alternate the Dealer clockwise after each game].</li>
<li>As a group, help Shuffle the Deck, then the Dealer <a href="#glossary:deal">Deals</span></strong></a> Each Player 7 Cards Face-Down [watch out for the Face-Up Trap Class Chaos Cards – return them randomly to the Deck before continuing to <a href="#glossary:deal">Deal</span></strong></a>].</li>
<li>Place the remainder of the Cards Face-Down as a Deck.</li>
<li>Place the <strong>"Direction Of Play" [Blue/Clockwise Side]</strong> and the <strong>"Play To The Side Of The Discards"</strong> Setup Cards face-up on either side of the Discards, where everyone can see them [Remember to Flip the <strong>"Direction Of Play"</strong> Setup Card it every time a Reverse Effect Activates].</li>
<li>If using the Core Extension, place "Recycle" Setup Card Face-Up next to the Deck and Discards, where all Players can reach it.</li>
<li>Flip the top Card of the Deck over to begin the Discards:
<ol type="a">
<li>If any Action Class Cards are turned over at this stage, follow their rules.</li>
<li>If any Chaos Card is turned over [including if a Trap Class Chaos Card is revealed in doing so], return it randomly to the Deck and Flip another Card.</li>
</ol>
</li>
</ol>

<!-- HOW TO PLAY -->
<div id="crazy-8s-how-to-play">
<div class="rules-box">
<h4><span style="color: #3b82f6;">HOW TO PLAY</span></h4>
<div class="rules-box">
<ol>
<li>Play starts clockwise from the Dealer.</li>
<li>On your Turn, you must play a minimum of 1 Card from your Hand by matching it to the Card on top of Discards either by Colour or Symbol, or by playing a Chaos Card [if able].
<div class="note-box">
<strong>NOTE:</strong>
<ul>
<li>Chaos Cards can affect how many Cards you can play on your Turn.</li>
</ul>
</div>
</li>
<li>If you don’t have a playable Card, as outlined above, you must <a href="#glossary:draw">Draw</span></strong></a> a Card from the Deck.
<div class="note-box" id="pick-up-put-down">
<strong>PICK-UP/PUT-DOWN RULE:</strong>
<ul>
<li>If the Card you picked up is a playable Coloured Card, feel free to play it now. Otherwise, play moves on to the next person.</li>
</ul>
</div>
<div class="restriction-box">
<strong>PICK-UP/PUT-DOWN RESTRICTION:</strong>
<ul>
<li>Chaos Cards cannot be played via the Pick-Up/Put-Down Rule [Some exceptions apply].</li>
</ul>
</div>
</li>
<li>You may also choose not to play a playable Card from your Hand [such as keeping a Chaos Card up your sleeve for later]. If so, you must <a href="#glossary:draw">Draw</span></strong></a> a Card from the Deck. The Pick-Up/Put-Down Rule applies here as well; however, you may not play any other Card from your Hand after <a href="#glossary:draw">Drawing</span></strong></a>.</li>
</ol>

<!-- VICTORY CONDITIONS -->
<div id="crazy-8s-victory-conditions">
<div class="rules-box">
<h4><span style="color: #3b82f6;">VICTORY CONDITIONS</span></h4>
<div class="rules-box">
<div id="victory-conditions-01">
<ol>
<li>While placing your second last card, choose whether to indicate that you have one card left to the group or not by stating “Solo!”
<ul>
<li>While it is common courtesy to announce your last Card, I am not forcing a Rule to do so. Therefore, you are encouraged to use the tactic of hiding this information.</li>
<li>However, if you do not and another Player notices before the next Player plays or DRAWS a Card, they can still call you out on it by saying “Solo!”. If they do, you must <a href="#glossary:draw">Draw</span></strong></a> 2 Cards to yourself.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>
</div>
<div id="victory-conditions-02">
<ol start="2">
<li>Possessing no cards at the end of your Turn.
<ul>
<li>If at the end of your Turn you still have no Cards, the game ends and you win!</li>
<li>Points can be scored if desired [see: <a href="#crazy-8s-scoring">Scoring</a>], and play can restart if desired.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>
</div>
<div id="victory-conditions-03">
<ol start="3">
<li>If the last card played in a round has a <a href="#glossary:draw">Draw</span></strong></a> or <a href="#glossary:deal">Deal Effect</span></strong></a>, continue play until someone is forced to <a href="#glossary:draw">Draw</span></strong></a> cards.
<ul>
<li>Someone must <a href="#glossary:draw">Draw</span></strong></a> from the last <a href="#glossary:draw">Draw</span></strong></a> or <a href="#glossary:deal">Deal Effect</span></strong></a> Card. Until that happens, your Win is not guaranteed...</li>
<li>If you play a <a href="#glossary:draw">Draw</span></strong></a> or <a href="#glossary:deal">Deal Effect</span></strong></a> Card as your last Card and a Player/s <a href="#glossary:respond-in-kind">Respond in Kind</span></strong></a>, and in doing so, brings the <a href="#glossary:draw">Draw</span></strong></a> or <a href="#glossary:deal">Deal Effect</span></strong></a> back to you, with nothing to defend yourself with you must bear the consequences of the <a href="#glossary:draw">Draw</span></strong></a> or <a href="#glossary:deal">Deal Effect</span></strong></a> you initiated, cancelling your Win.
<div class="note-box">
<strong>NOTE:</strong>
<ul>
<li>There is also the possibility of the Drawer obtaining <strong>"Anti-Fun"</strong> for example, that can still be used to negate your Win. It is also a possibility that while <a href="#glossary:draw">Drawing</span></strong></a> with certain Expansions that a Group Targeting <a href="#glossary:draw">Draw Effect</span></strong></a> Chaos Card could charge everyone, including you, Card/s that would also cancel your win.</li>
</ul>
</div>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>
</div><div id="victory-conditions-04">
<ol start="4">
<li>The game ends Without a Clear Winner.
<ol type="a">
<li>In any situation without a clear Winner:
<ul>
<li>The Player with the least number of Cards Wins.</li>
<li>If more than one Player has equal Cards to Win, They Draw.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>
</li>
</ol>
</div>

<!-- SCORING: WINNING THE GAME -->
<div id="crazy-8s-scoring-winning">
<div class="rules-box">
<h4><span style="color: #3b82f6;">SCORING - WINNING THE GAME</span></h4>
<div class="rules-box">
<div class="note-box">
<strong>NOTE:</strong> The number of Chaos Cards will speed up Points collection.
</div>
<div class="rules-box">
<ul>
<li><strong>OPTION 1: Standard Game:</strong>
<ul>
<li>The winner is the first Player to reach 3,000 points.</li>
<li>The more Players you have the longer the game takes, but the more points you’ll earn for winning. So, keeping a set Score Target allows for faster “Scoring Wins” the more Players you have, speeding up total play time.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</div>
<div class="rules-box">
<ul>
<li><strong>OPTION 2: Extended Game:</strong>
<ul>
<li>The winner is the first Player to reach 3,000-7,000 points depending on the number of Players.</li>
<li>Starting at 3,000 for 3 Players, increase the Victory Points required by 500 for every additional player [e.g.: 4 Players will be 3,500, 5 Players will be 4,000, 6 Players will be 4,500, etc... but keep a Maximum of 7,000 even if playing with more than the recommended maximum of 10 Players].</li>
<li>
The more Players you have the longer the game takes, but the more points you’ll earn for winning. However, for a serious game, increasing the Score Target allows for incrementally slower “Scoring Wins” the more Players you have, extending total play time.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</div>
<div class="rules-box">
<ul>
<li><strong>OPTION 3: Last Player Standing:</strong>
<ul>
<li>There is no winner for Rounds. As play continues and Players Go Out, they are permanently exited from the game [as opposed to Remove from Game Effects of Expansion Chaos Cards (e.g. : Assassin from the Doomsday Expansion) ] until the last Player is left.</li>
<li>A Point is given for every Player left in the game when you go out.</li>
<li>The Last Player Standing has Points deducted based on the Number of other Players in the Game.<div class="note-box">
<strong>NOTES:</strong>
<ul>
</li>Players cannot obtain a Negative Score.</li>
</ul>
</div>
</li>
<li>Play continues until a Player Scores the Required Total [the recommendation is 3 x the Number of Players in the game].</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</div>
<div class="rules-box">
<ul>
<li><strong>TOURNAMENT:</strong>
<ul>
<li>The first Player to reach one of the above Point requirements earns 1 Credit. Rounds progress until one Player earns a total of 3 Credits [for the truly devoted, you could always agree to a larger figure ahead of playing].</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</div>

<!-- IMPORTANT NOTES SECTION -->
<div id="crazy-8s-important-notes">
<div class="rules-box">
<h4><span style="color: #3b82f6;">IMPORTANT NOTES</span></h4>
<div class="rules-box">
<div id="crazy-8s-important-notes-01">
<ol>
<li><strong>Never Re-Shuffle the Discards into the Deck as this has the potential for the game to not end.</strong>
<ul>
<li>If the Deck runs out of Drawable Cards, the Game Ends and everyone loses! [e.g.: (01-A) Soloverse (Crazy 8's) (Game Rulebook): Victory Conditions: Rule 5].
<div class="tip-box">
<strong>TIP:</strong>
<ul>
<li>“The <strong>"Recycle"</strong> Setup Card from the Core Extension can give you options here.”</li>
</ul>
</div>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<div id="crazy-8s-important-notes-02">
<li><strong>There is a difference between <a href="#glossary:deal">Deal Effects</span></strong></a> and <a href="#glossary:draw">Draw Effects</span></strong></a>:</strong>
<ul>
<li>All <a href="#glossary:deal">Deal Effects</span></strong></a> [including the initial <a href="#glossary:deal">Deal</span></strong></a>] ignore all Trap Effects [return them to the Deck if Revealed].</li>
<li>All <a href="#glossary:draw">Draw Effects</span></strong></a> follow Trap Effects.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</div>
<div id="crazy-8s-important-notes-03">
<li><strong>In some cases, Card Effects can Override and/or Delay others.</strong>
<ul>
<li>When this happens, the last Card Always takes Priority.
<div class="example-box">
<strong>EXAMPLES:</strong>
<ul>
<li>If the <strong>"Stun"</strong> Chaos Card is in play and <strong>"Vaccine"</strong> is played, the <strong>"Stun"</strong> Chaos Card cannot Deactivate [and Stun its target’s Turn] until <strong>"Vaccine"</strong> has finished.</li>
<li>If the <strong>"Vaccine"</strong> Chaos Card is in play and its User <a href="#glossary:miss-their-turn">Misses Thier Next Turn</span></strong></a>, the <strong>"Vaccine"</strong> Chaos Card cannot Deactivate [allowing Chaos Cards to be played/activated again] due to its User not having a <a href="#glossary:playable-turn">Playable Turn</span></strong></a> that round.</li>
</ul>
</div>
</li>
</ul>
</div>
<div id="crazy-8s-important-notes-04">
<li><strong>Playable Turn Notes:</strong>
<ul>
<li>A <a href="#glossary:playable-turn">Playable Turn</span></strong></a> describes a Turn that the Player can freely choose for themselves what Card to play.</li>
<li>A <a href="#glossary:playable-turn">Playable Turn</span></strong></a> does not describe a Turn where a Player is facing Card Effects.
<div class="example-box">
<strong>EXAMPLE:</strong>
<ul>
<li>Such as having their Turn Skipped or needing to <a href="#glossary:respond-in-kind">Respond in Kind</span></strong></a> to <a href="#glossary:stackable">Stacked</span></strong></a> <a href="#glossary:draw">Draw Effects</span></strong></a>.</li>
</ul>
</div>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</div>
<div id="crazy-8s-important-notes-05">
<li><strong>Turn Order x Discard Timing:</strong>
<ul>
<li>It is important to follow the Turn Order when such events like Group Discarding occurs.
<div class="example-box">
<strong>EXAMPLE:</strong>
<ul>
<li>Playing the <strong>"Discard Chaos Cards"</strong> Chaos Card when you only have Chaos Cards left and you expect to win. Technically, in most cases, the User is last to Discard. Meaning, another Player could Discard their Chaos Cards and beat you to the Win.</li>
</ul>
</div>
<div class="tip-box">
<strong>TIP:</strong>
<ul>
<li>“Unless someone else who hasn’t had to Discard yet still has an <strong>"Anti-Fun"</strong> Chaos Card.”</li>
</ul>
</div>
<div class="example-box">
<strong>EXAMPLE:</strong>
<ul>
<li>Chaos Cards that force all Players to Group Discard 1 more Cards. Observing the Turn Order grants the last Player [in most cases the User] the advantage to choose the Card that continues play.</li>
</ul>
</div>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</div>
<div id="crazy-8s-important-notes-06">
<li><strong>Trap Class Chaos Card Notes:</strong>
<ul>
<li>All Trap Class Chaos Cards are Dual-Sided and therefore appear Face-Up in the Deck when Shuffled.</li>
<li>When revealed, remove it from the Deck temporarily and follow its Effects.</li>
<li>All are Reshuffled back into the Deck after all Effects have been resolved.</li>
<li>If any are revealed while <a href="#glossary:deal">Dealing</span></strong></a> [including during the initial Flip to form the Discards], return them randomly to the Deck.</li>
<li>Some have Instant Effects that must be Finalised before continuing [e.g.: <strong>"Mercy"</strong>] and therefore prevent additional Trap Class Chaos Cards from being Activated.</li>
<li>All Trap Class Chaos Cards CANNOT be <a href="#glossary:counter">Countered</span></strong></a>, unless specified [including Trap Class Chaos Cards from Expansions].</li>
</ul>
</li>
</div>
<div id="crazy-8s-important-notes-07">
<li><strong>Multiple Trap Class Chaos Card Activations:</strong>
<ul>
<li>If multiple Trap Class Chaos Cards are triggered during <a href="#glossary:draw">Drawing</span></strong></a>, place the latest Trap Class Chaos Card on top of the previous and complete its Effects first.</li>
<li>Place each Trap Class Chaos Card in a separate “used pile” as their Effects are Finalised before returning to the previous Trap Class Chaos Card.</li>
<li>Then Shuffle all Trap Class Chaos Cards back into the Deck once all Effects have been Finalised.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</div>
<div id="crazy-8s-important-notes-08">
<li><strong>Some Cards have Secondary Effect Icons:</strong>
<ul>
<li>Their details will list these Effects First, in Order of Priority and Second, in the Order they Happen.
<div class="example-box">
<strong>EXAMPLES:</strong>
<ul>
<li>In the case of the <strong>"Chaos Draw +4 Reverse"</strong> Chaos Card, the <a href="#glossary:draw">Draw +4 Effect</span></strong></a> is the Primary Effect, while the Reverse Effect happens first.</li>
<li>After the Direction of Play Reverses, the previous Player must deal with the Primary <a href="#glossary:draw">Draw Effect</span></strong></a> by <a href="#glossary:draw">Drawing</span></strong></a> or <a href="#glossary:respond-in-kind">Responding in Kind</span></strong></a>.</li>
<li>However, once the Primary <a href="#glossary:draw">Draw Effect</span></strong></a> has been resolved, the next Player can act upon the Secondary Reverse Effect.</li>
<li>Even if the <strong>"Chaos Draw +4 Reverse"</strong> was called Blue, the next Player can play any Coloured <strong>"Reverse"</strong>.</li>
</ul>
</div>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</div>
<div id="crazy-8s-important-notes-09">
<li><strong>Counter Class Chaos Card Levels:</strong>
<ul>
<li>Each Counter Class Chaos Card will have its own Level [e.g.: Lv1, Lv3, etc]. </li>
<li>With some exceptions, any Counter Class Chaos Card CAN be <a href="#glossary:counter">Countered</span></strong></a> back at you, IF the Responding Counter Class Chaos Card is the Same Level or Higher.
<div class="example-box">
<strong>EXAMPLES:</strong>
<ul>
<li>Compare <strong>"Nou"</strong> [a Lv2 Counter Class Chaos Card from the Core Game] and <strong>"Not Me"</strong> [a Lv3 Counter Class Chaos Card from the Core Extension].</li>
<li><strong>"Not Me"</strong> [Lv3] CAN Counter Itself &amp; <strong>"Nou"</strong> [Lv2].</li>
<li>Whereas <strong>"Nou"</strong> [Lv2] CAN Counter Itself but CANNOT Counter <strong>"Not Me"</strong> [Lv3].</li>
</ul>
</div>
<div class="advanced-box">
<strong>IMPORTANT:</strong>
<ul>
<li>Keep in mind that there are Specifically <a href="#glossary:counter">Countered</span></strong></a> situations that contradict this Important Note.</li>
</ul>
</div>
<div class="example-box">
<strong>EXAMPLE:</strong>
<ul>
<li><strong>"Anti-Fun"</strong> [a Level 1 Counter Class Chaos Card from the Core Game]. Despite all other Counter Class Chaos Cards having a higher Level, only <strong>"Nou"</strong> can Counter <strong>"Anti-Fun"</strong> if played as your last Card.</li>
</ul>
</div>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</div>
</ol>

<!-- TURN OVERVIEW DEFINITIONS SECTION -->
<div id="crazy-8s-turn-overview-definitions">
<div class="rules-box">
<h4><span style="color: #3b82f6;">TURN OVERVIEW DEFINITIONS</span></h4>
<div class="rules-box">
<div class="tip-box">
[“These Turn Overview Definitions will almost never be needed, but in instances where disputes arise, they will come in Handy”].
</div>
<div class="rules-box">
<h3 class="rulebook-subheading">OPENING PHASE:</h3>
<p><strong>Definition:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>After the previous Player’s End Phase, but before your Turn actually starts.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Required For:</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>Carry Over Effects:
<ul>
<li>Some Card Effects Carry Over between Player's Turns and Activate during the Opening Phase of the next Player’s Turn [“As in you face them before your Turn actually starts”].
<div class="example-box">
<strong>EXAMPLE:</strong>
<ul>
<li><strong>"Draw +2"</strong> &amp; <strong>"Chaos Draw +4"</strong> from the Core Game.</li>
</ul>
</div>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Activate Before Starting Your Turn:
<ul>
<li>Certain Chaos Cards activate in the Opening Phase if they state: Activate before you start a <a href="#glossary:playable-turn">Playable Turn</span></strong></a>. Your turn’s Main Phase only starts after such Chaos Cards’ Effects are activated.
<div class="example-box">
<strong>EXAMPLE:</strong>
<ul>
<li>A lot of Expansion Chaos Cards use this Effect such as the Royal Knight Class <strong>"Jack’s Knight"</strong> from the Oblivion Expansion. It changes the rules for your Main Phase, allowing 3 playable Cards to be played instead of just 1.</li>
</ul>
</div>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</div>
<div class="rules-box">
<h3 class="rulebook-subheading">MAIN PHASE:</h3>
<p><strong>Definition:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Basically, your actual Turn; the Card or Cards you play.</li>
</ul>
</div>
<div class="rules-box">
<h3 class="rulebook-subheading">END PHASE:</h3>
<p><strong>Definition:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>The last step in your Turn; it sits between your Main Phase and the next Player’s Opening Phase.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Required For:</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>Effect Finalisation: The last step in your Turn after playing a Card.
<div class="example-box">
<strong>EXAMPLE:</strong>
<ul>
<li>After activating a Chaos Card, you will usually need to Choose a Colour to Continue, and this designates the start of your End Phase.</li>
</ul>
</div>
</li>
<li>Victory Conditions: Victory Conditions are checked during this Phase.</li>
<li>Chaos Card Activation: “Most cards do not use this, but one does come to mind.”
<div class="example-box">
<strong>EXAMPLE:</strong>
<ul>
<li>That example is the Counter Class Chaos Card: <strong>"Anti-Fun"</strong>, from the Core Game. It is technically during your End Phase, in response to your Victory Condition of No Cards Left, that any Opponent can use it to force you to <a href="#glossary:deal">Deal</span></strong></a> 5 extra Cards to yourself. At this point, your Victory Condition checks for No Cards Left, finds Cards and cancels itself, completing your Turn’s End Phase.</li>
</ul>
</div>
</li>
<li>Carry Over Effects: Many Effects are forwarded onto the next Player’s Opening Phase [see: Opening Phase above].</li>
</li>Such Carry Over Effects cause the Next Player to not have a <a href="#glossary:playable-turn">Playable Turn</span></strong></a>
</ol>
</div>
</div>

<div id="crazy-8s-glossary">
<div class="rules-box">
<h4><span style="color: #3b82f6;">GLOSSARY</span></h4>
<div class="rules-box">
<div id="crazy-8s-glossary-draw">
<ol start="1">
<li>DRAW:
<ul>
<li>Take Cards from the Deck, while following Trap Effects.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>
</div>
<div id="crazy-8s-glossary-deal">
<ol start="2">
<li>DEAL:
<ul>
<li>Take Cards from the Deck, while ignoring all Trap Effects.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>
</div>
<div id="crazy-8s-glossary-playable-turn">
<ol start="3">
<li>PLAYABLE TURN:
<ul>
<li>Start Your Turn while NOT facing Card Effects, allowing You to chose any Card You want to Play.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>
</div>
<div id="crazy-8s-glossary-miss-their-turn">
<ol start="4">
<li><strong>MISS THEIR TURN:</strong>
<ul>
<li>A Card Effect that prevents You from Playing that Turn.</li>
<li>Generally additional Effects like Draw Effects are added, but not always.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>
</div>
<div id="crazy-8s-glossary-targeted">
<ol start="5">
<li><strong>TARGETED:</strong>
<ul>
<li>A Card Effect that affects another Player or Card Effect.</li>
<li>Targeting Types:
<ul>
<li>Self Targeting: Only You are affected.</li>
<li>Next Targeting: The Next Player in Play Order.</li>
<li>Group Targeting: Everyone in the Game.</li>
<li>Specific Targeting: A Specific Player of Your Choice.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Unique Targeting Types:
<ul>
<li>Victory Targeting: This is what <strong>"Anti-Fun"</strong> uses to stop someone from Winning.</li>
<li>Unknown Targeting: Multiple Targeting Types available.</li>
<li>Assassin Targeting: Only Targets the Effects of the Assassin Chaos Card.</li>
<li>Restriction Targeting: Targets the Restrictions of Chaos Cards.</li>
<li>Conceptual Targeting: Targets the Rules of the Game.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>
</div>
<div id="crazy-8s-glossary-stackable">
<ol start="6">
<li><strong>STACKABLE:</strong>
<ul>
<li>A Card that can be Played to continue and build the Effect onto another Player.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>
</div>
<div id="crazy-8s-glossary-respond-in-kind">
<ol start="7">
<li>RESPOND IN KIND:
<ul>
<li>The action of placing a Playable Card that allows You to STACK an Effect onto another Player or COUNTER that Effect back at the previous Player.</li>
<li>Responding in Kind is NOT considered a Playable Turn.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>
</div>
<div id="crazy-8s-glossary-counter">
<ol start="8">
<li><strong>COUNTER:</strong>
<ul>
<li>A Card Effect that generally throws a COUNTERABLE Effect back at the Player that Targeted You.</li>
<li>Some COUNTER Effects prevent certain Game Conditions or Trap Effects.
<div class="example-box">
<strong>EXAMPLE:</strong>
<ul>
<li><strong>"ANTI-FUN"</strong> allows You to cancel a Player's Win.</li>
<li><strong>"CURE"</strong> allows You to cancel a Trap Class Chaos Card from any of the: Bio-Hazard, Patient Zero or Pandemic Expansions and remove it from the Game.</li>
</ul>
</div>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>
</div>
