<li>Singularity Expansion [SOL/OVE/RSE]</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</div>
<div class="tip-box">
<strong>TIP:</strong>
<ul>
<li>Playing with certain Expansions can heavily drain the Deck of Cards.</li>
<li>If Playing with any Draw Effect Companion Expansion Sets such as:
<ul>
<li>The Apocalypse / Warzone / Devastation / Catastrophe Companion Expansion Set</li>
<li>OR</li>
<li>The Bio-Hazard / Patient Zero / Pandemic / Containment Companion Expansion Set</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>It is recommended to use at least 8 Colour Sets.</li>
<li>As an Exception, if Playing with BOTH of those Companion Expansion Sets, it is recommended to use at least 10 Colour Sets.</li>
</ul>
